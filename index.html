    How the weekly challenge works
    1. Free to enter. One ranked run per player each week (Mon 00:00 – Sun 23:59, Lagos time).
    2. Same questions for all. Everyone gets the same 15 questions; they are held on our server and shown one at a time.
    3. Highest money wins. Ties are broken by more correct answers, then your fastest total answer time.
    4. Server-checked. Answers and timing are checked on the server — scores can't be edited in your browser.
    5. Claim within 14 days. The winner is verified, then paid by airtime or bank transfer. Suspicious runs are reviewed and can be disqualified.
    6. One person, one entry. Sharing answers or using bots or multiple devices gets you removed.
    7. Read the full contest rules and privacy notice.
